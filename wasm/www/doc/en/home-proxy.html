Chiaki Home Proxy

Small program on the PC next to the PS4/PS5. It relays Remote Play and LAN discovery. Outbound connection: no ports to open on the router.

Web only

Hidden in Electron, which already has LAN.

Setup

  1. Sign in on the site (same account as the agent).
  2. Settings → Config → Chiaki Home Proxy → Download for Windows or Linux (macOS if published).
  3. Run it, Chiaki email + password. Leave the window open.
  4. On the site: Approve. Trust lasts 12 hours.
  5. LAN discovery starts.

Disconnect in Config: the agent will not reconnect by itself.

What you get

  • 192.168.x from the Internet
  • Auto PS4/PS5 discovery
  • 9295 test from the home PC
  • Stream without NAT

Another Chiaki account cannot see your LAN.

Troubleshooting

  • Approve the prompt; pending agents do not relay LAN.
  • Relaunch the exe after an update (banner 1.1+).
  • Do not put the console IP in Custom proxy.
  • Windows Firewall: allow the exe on the private network.
