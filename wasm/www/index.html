      Share stream

      While you stream, people with the link receive video, audio and, if you allow it, the virtual pad or a physical gamepad. The virtual pad uses your theme.

      Your own game stays live. Viewers are a few seconds behind: a frame is sent only after the screen was checked with no keyword, so pause happens before they see the word.

      Separate several keywords with a comma, a semicolon or a new line.
